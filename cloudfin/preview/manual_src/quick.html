Welcome

AERIA OS is an operating system for the Octatrack MKII, written by sadnoise. It takes the place of the instrument's own and turns the same panel, the same sockets and the same sound processor into a different instrument with two faces: a live multi-effect built around a sky, and an eight-track sequencer.

The skyWhat you play into inputs A to D runs through a chain of up to seven effects. The weather moves their knobs and lends effects of its own. A loop, a ring of one-shots called stars, and balloons you record yourself feed the chain as well.
The sequencerA groove box: eight tracks on the sixteen trigs. Each track has a voice (a sample, one of four small synthesizers, or MIDI alone), two filters and room for one effect. Every step can have its own values: parameter locks.
Shared by bothThe master section (a freeze delay, a four-band EQ, bass mono and a limiter on the main output), the tempo, sixteen projects, the card, USB audio and the screensavers.

[FUNC] + [MIDI] goes from one face to the other. The popup says ``SEQUENCER`` or ``AERIA``.

Switching on and finding your way

  1. Connect Main Out L R to your mixer or speakers, or plug in headphones.
  2. Connect something to play to inputs A B: a synthesizer, a drum machine, a microphone preamp.
  3. Put in the CompactFlash card if you have samples on one. AERIA OS plays without it.
  4. Switch on. The intro paints the logo and writes BY SADNOISE; the sea rises and the sky opens. The intro is silent. Any key skips it.
[[G2]] intro1 | The brush paints the logo intro3 | The sea rises, and the sky opens [[/G]]

AERIA OS comes back as you left it: it keeps what you are doing in the unit's battery-backed memory, the sound about two seconds after a change and the sequence at every touch. If you switched off in the sequencer, it starts in the sequencer, stopped. The very first time, the chain holds PITCH, GRAINS, RING and SMEAR in slots 1, 3, 4 and 5, all dry: you hear your sound as it comes in.

First sound in the sky

Play something into inputs A B and listen on the main outputs or the headphones. Each step shows the keys to press and what the screen should look like afterwards.

[[STEP 1 | SRC TRIG1-7=slots | qs_sky_chain]]

Open the chain. Press [SRC]. The seven slots hang on a string; slot 1, PITCH, is selected. You hear your sound as it is, because every effect is dry.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 2 | TRIG3 F=MIX | qs_sky_mix]]

Bring an effect in. Press [TRIG 3] to select GRAINS. Turn ((F)), MIX, up to about half. Your sound is now cut into grains.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 3 | A=SIZE B=DENS C=POS D=SPRY E=PTCH | chain_popup_ms]]

Play its knobs. ((A)) to ((E)) are the effect's other knobs. The popup reads in real units. Hold [FUNC] for the effect's second set.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 4 | TRIG6 BANK=shelf YES UP DOWN LEVEL | shelf]]

Pick another effect. Press [TRIG 6], an empty slot, then [BANK] (or [YES]) for the shelf: the list of effects. Choose with ((LEVEL)) or [UP] [DOWN] and press [YES]. [NO] goes back.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 5 | AMP=SKY TRIG3=RAIN TRIG1=CLEAR | qs_sky_rain]]

Change the weather. Press [AMP] for the SKY page, then [TRIG 3]: RAIN. The effects' knobs glide to rainier places and a low-pass eases in. [TRIG 1] clears the sky again.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 6 | MIX=MASTER F=WET TRIGS=time | qs_sky_delay]]

Add the master's delay. Press [MIX] for the MASTER page. Turn ((F)), WET, up. The sixteen trigs set the delay's time in sixteenth notes.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 7 | XFADER=freeze | master_freeze]]

Freeze it. Push the crossfader to B. The popup says ``FREEZE ON``: what is in the delay goes round for as long as you like. Bring the crossfader back and it lets go.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 8 | SCENEA SCENEB XFADER KNOBS SRC | scene_knob]]

Make two scenes. Press [SRC]. Hold [SCENE A] and turn a knob; hold [SCENE B] and turn the same knob somewhere else. Now the crossfader moves that knob between the two places.

[[/STEP]]
Effects start dry. An effect's MIX (knob ((F))) says how much of it you hear. The weather never moves a MIX. An effect taken from the shelf comes in at its own default MIX.
Good to know on CHAIN. [FUNC] + a trig or track key bypasses a slot. Push a knob to hand it back to the weather. The first line of the shelf, a dash, empties the slot.

The weather

The weather is the heart of the sky. Each weather is on or off by itself, and several can be on at once. On the SKY, MIX and GAIN pages the trigs are the weathers:

[[TRIGS 1121111111110001 | The trigs on SKY, MIX and GAIN. Here RAIN is on. 1 turns every weather off; 12 sends one twister across; 16 is a lightning strike | 1=CLEAR 2=CLOUDY 3=RAIN 4=STORM 5=SNOW 6=HEAT 7=FOG 8=AUTO 9=WIND 10=HAIL 11=AUR. 12=TWIST. 16=bolt]] [[G3]] sky_rain | RAIN sky_storm | STORM, at a lightning strike sky_snow | SNOW: the sea freezes flat sky_fog | FOG sky_aurora | AURORA, at night sky_twister | A TWISTER half way across [[/G]]

It moves the knobs

CLOUDY, RAIN, STORM, SNOW, HEAT and FOG each have a character, and set the knobs of every effect in the chain to suit: RAIN wetter and longer, STORM wild: it throws every free knob of every effect somewhere new every few seconds, and its lightning drops effects in and takes them out again, SNOW very long and dark (it freezes SMEAR), HEAT dry and bright. It goes by the knob's name: SIZE, TIME, DECY, FDBK, TONE, RATE, RES, WDTH, DPTH, SMR, FRZ, GRN, DENS, WIDE, TILT, BRIT, GATE, DTUN, MOD and FOLW follow the weather.

  • A knob follows the weather until you turn it. Then it is yours. Push the knob on CHAIN to give it back.
  • MIX never follows the weather.
  • SNOW and HEAT cannot be on together.
  • The weather never touches the master section.

It lends effects

The weather also acts on the sound directly. RAIN and STORM ease a low-pass in; thunder rolls it down; in a STORM a lightning strike lends a random effect to the last empty slot for 45 to 90 seconds.

FOG, WIND, HAIL, AURORA and the TWISTER each bring an effect of their own. While the weather lasts, the sky puts it into the last empty slot of the chain and fades it in; the popup says, for example, ``AERIA WIND IN``. When the weather goes, the effect fades and the slot is emptied.

  • No empty slot, or no room left in the sound processor (``NO ROOM`` ``DSP FULL``): the sky still shows the weather, and its sound is left out.
  • WIND and FOG are never on together, nor HAIL and AURORA.
WSND is the switch for all of it. WSND is knob ((F)) on the MIX page ([FX1]). At ON, the weather is heard: its filters, the thunder, and the effects it lends. At OFF the sky shows the weather and still moves the knobs, but nothing of it is heard and nothing is lent. If a weather seems to do nothing, look at WSND, then for an empty slot.

Recording: balloons and samples

In the sky you record into balloons. A balloon is a recording of up to 15 seconds that floats in the sky and sings as a cloud of long grains, through the chain. There are three.

[[STEP 1 | REC1=A_B REC2=C_D REC3=sky | balloon_filling]]

Catch, and let go. Press [REC1] to record inputs A B into balloon 1, [REC2] for inputs C D into balloon 2, or [REC3] to catch the sky itself (the chain's output) into balloon 3. The popup says ``CATCHING`` and the balloon swells on the water. Press the same key again and it floats up.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 2 | LFO=SOURCE TRIG1=LOOP TRIG2=STARS TRIG3=BALLN | source]]

Let it sing. Press [LFO] for SOURCE and [TRIG 3]: BALLOONS on. Every floating balloon now sings into the start of the chain, so the effects work on it too.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 3 | PART=BALLOON KNOBS FUNC RECS | balloon_popup]]

Shape it, or pop it. [PART] opens the BALLOON page: SIZE, SPRY, PTCH, DENS, POS and SEND. [FUNC] + the recorder key pops a balloon; its recording is gone.

[[/STEP]]
The balloons' recordings are not kept when the power goes off, and a project does not hold them.

Samples from the card

The other two sources of the sky, and the sequencer's kit, play WAV files from the CompactFlash card. AERIA OS only reads the card; to put files on it, use USB DISK MODE (section 10). In the card browser [UP] [DOWN] or ((LEVEL)) move, [YES] opens or loads, [NO] goes back.

First beat in the sequencer

Before you start: on a computer, put eight or more short WAV files in a folder on the card. Number them so that the kick lists first: 01 kick, 02 clap, 03 hat and so on.

[[STEP 1 | FUNC MIDI | qs_seq_empty]]

Go to the sequencer. Press [FUNC] + [MIDI]. The popup says ``SEQUENCER``. Eight rows of dots: eight empty tracks. Track 1 is in hand: its row is the tall one.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 2 | SRC FUNC YES UP DOWN | seq_kit]]

Load a kit. Press [SRC], then [FUNC] + [YES]. The card opens. Move to your folder with [UP] [DOWN] and press [FUNC] + [YES] on it. Tracks 1 to 8 play its first eight files.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 3 | T1 TRIG1 TRIG5 TRIG9 TRIG13 | qs_seq_kick]]

Set a kick. Press [T1]; while stopped you hear the track as you pick it. Tap [TRIG 1], [TRIG 5], [TRIG 9] and [TRIG 13]: a kick on every beat.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 4 | PLAY STOP TEMPO LEVEL | qs_seq_play]]

Play. Press [PLAY]. The step being played is lit on the screen and on the trigs, and <<TEMPO>> flashes on the beat. Tap [TEMPO] to set the speed, or hold it and turn ((LEVEL)).

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 5 | T2 T3 TRIG5 TRIG13 TRIG3 TRIG7 TRIG11 TRIG15 | qs_seq_hats]]

Add a clap and hats. Press [T2] and tap [TRIG 5] and [TRIG 13]. Press [T3] and tap [TRIG 3], [TRIG 7], [TRIG 11] and [TRIG 15].

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 6 | TRIG15=hold E=RTCH | qs_seq_ratchet]]

Roll one hat. Hold [TRIG 15]: the knobs become that step's own. Turn ((E)), RTCH, to 3 and let go. That hat now plays three times inside its step.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 7 | TRIG7=hold FX1=FLT B=LP | qs_seq_lock]]

Lock a step. Hold [TRIG 7], press [FX1], and turn ((B)), LP, down. Let go. That one hat is darker than the others: a parameter lock.

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 8 | FX2=FX YES UP DOWN | qs_seq_fxlist]]

Give the track an effect. Press [FX2], then [YES]. Choose ECHO with [UP] [DOWN] and press [YES]. The echo's six knobs are now on ((A)) to ((F)).

[[/STEP]] [[STEP 9 | BANK=mixer LEVEL T1-8 TRIG1-8=mute TRIG9-16=solo | qs_seq_mixer]]

Balance. Press [BANK] for the mixer. Press a track key and turn ((LEVEL)). Trigs 1 to 8 mute, 9 to 16 solo. [BANK] again closes it.

Eight knob pages for the track in hand

MACH on the SRC page chooses the track's machine: SMPL (a sample), SUB (a sine with a falling pitch), FM (two sines), NOIS (noise), PLUK (a plucked string) or MIDI (no sound: the track only plays another instrument). ((LEVEL)) is the volume of the track in hand on every page. A track is 1 to 64 steps long (LEN on PTN); [LEFT] [RIGHT] turn its pages of sixteen. The sequence is kept in the unit's memory at every touch. While the sequencer is showing, the sky's chain and sources are not heard; inputs A to D pass to the output dry.

USB: recording into a computer, and the card

Connect the USB socket to a computer. AERIA OS appears as one device named AERIA. It needs no driver. Its audio interface sends sound from the unit to the computer at 44.1 kHz, 24-bit, in one of two formats. The computer chooses.

Six channels

The format a computer picks by itself. Channels 1 to 4 are inputs A, B, C, D as they come in; 5 and 6 are the main output, exactly what leaves the Main Out sockets. Record your dry playing and AERIA's result at once.

Eighteen channels: stems

Channels 1 and 2 are the main output; 3 to 18 are eight stereo stems, one for each sequencer track, for mixing a recorded jam afterwards.

  • A track with no effect is mono: its stem is the same on both channels.
  • The delay's echoes are in the main pair only. A muted track sends nothing.

Choosing 18 channels on a Mac

  1. Open Audio MIDI Setup (in Applications, Utilities).
  2. In the list at the left, click AERIA.
  3. Under Input, open the Format menu and choose 18 ch.
  4. In your recording program, choose AERIA as the input device and make nine stereo tracks.
  • To go back, choose 6 ch in the same menu.
  • In the sky, the stems carry silence.
  • While stems stream, the screen is redrawn more slowly. The sound does not change.

USB DISK MODE: files onto the card

  1. In the sky, press [FX2] for SYSTEM, then [TRIG 1]. The card appears on the computer as a drive you can read and write.
  2. Copy your WAV files: loops, folders of one-shots, kits.
  3. Eject the drive on the computer first, then press [NO]. Only [NO] works on the panel meanwhile.

Notes out from a track

  1. In the sequencer, take a track in hand and press [MIDI].
  2. Turn ((A)), MCH, from OFF to the MIDI channel of your instrument.
  3. ((B)) NOTE is the note, ((C)) CHRD a chord on it, ((D)) GATE its length, ((E)) PROG a program change for that instrument.
  4. Set steps. Each one sends the note, at the step's velocity.

Hold a step, press [MIDI] and turn NOTE or CHRD: a lock. That is how a track plays a melody. For a track that only sends MIDI, turn MACH on SRC to MIDI.

Projects and saving

You do not have to save to carry on. AERIA OS keeps the saved state by itself: switch off and on, and everything is as it was.

A project is for coming back to a piece later: everything needed to play it again, under a name of up to twelve characters. There are sixteen, and a project holds both faces: the chain and every knob, the weathers, the scenes, the master, which samples are loaded, and all four patterns of the sequence. It keeps the places of its samples, not their sound: the card must hold the same files in the same folders.

  1. Press [PROJ], from either face.
  2. Pick one of the sixteen with a trig.
  3. To save: [FUNC] + [YES]. Type a name on the trigs (two letters to a trig; trig 14 digits, 15 space, 16 rubs out) and press [YES]. The popup says ``SAVED``.
  4. To load: [YES]. The popup says ``LOADED`` and the name.
  5. [NO] or [PROJ] closes the page.

Screensavers and games

When you have not touched a key or a knob for a while, a screensaver takes the screen. There are {{SAVER_COUNT}}: four games you can play, PADDLE, BRICKS, ROCKS and BLOCKS, and the rest to watch. The sound carries on underneath.

[[G4]] ss_paddle | PADDLE ss_bricks | BRICKS ss_rocks | ROCKS ss_blocks | BLOCKS [[/G]]

Settings are on SYSTEM, in the sky: press [FX2]. ((E)), SAVER, chooses OFF, one by name, or RANDOM; ((F)), WAIT, is the time before it starts, 1 to 60 minutes; [YES] starts it now. In a game, [LEFT] [RIGHT] [UP] [DOWN], [YES], ((A)) or ((LEVEL)) play; stop for three seconds and the game plays itself again. [NO] always ends a screensaver, and so does any other key or knob.
